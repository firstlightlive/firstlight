## DAILY MORNING (Do Every Day)

### 1. WAKE BEFORE SUNRISE — Brahma Muhurta (3:30-5:30 AM)
- **Source:** Rig Veda, Ayurveda (Charaka Samhita)
- **Why:** Cortisol-melatonin transition optimizes cellular repair. Vedas say "Brahma Muhurta is the time of Brahman — pure consciousness"
- **Do:** Wake at 3:15 AM (you already do this)
- **Time:** 0 min

### 2. TONGUE SCRAPING (Jihwa Prakshalana)
- **Source:** Ayurveda (Charaka Samhita Ch.5)
- **Why:** Removes overnight bacterial film (ama/toxins). Stimulates digestive fire (agni). Modern: reduces oral bacteria linked to heart disease + systemic inflammation
- **Do:** Copper tongue scraper, 7-10 strokes
- **Time:** 1 min

### 3. OIL PULLING (Gandusha) — Sesame or Coconut Oil
- **Source:** Charaka Samhita, Sushruta Samhita
- **Why:** Draws fat-soluble toxins. Strengthens jaw, prevents wrinkles around mouth. Modern: reduces Streptococcus mutans by 20%, anti-inflammatory
- **Do:** 1 tbsp sesame/coconut oil, swish 10-15 min, spit out
- **Time:** 10-15 min (while doing other things)

### 4. COLD WATER EXPOSURE / COLD SHOWER
- **Source:** Vedic (Snana), Buddhist monks, Wim Hof method
- **Why:** Activates brown fat (burns white fat), boosts norepinephrine 200-300%, increases cold shock proteins that repair cells. Atharva Veda prescribes cold water bathing for vigor
- **Do:** 2-5 min cold at end of shower
- **Time:** 2-5 min

### 5. PRANAYAMA — Alternate Nostril Breathing (Nadi Shodhana)
- **Source:** Yoga Sutras, Hatha Yoga Pradipika
- **Why:** Balances sympathetic/parasympathetic nervous system. Reduces cortisol (aging hormone). Activates vagus nerve → reduces inflammation. Modern: lowers biological age markers
- **Do:** 5 min, 10 rounds each side
- **Time:** 5 min

### 6. MEDITATION — Vipassana/Mindfulness
- **Source:** Buddhism (Satipatthana Sutta), Vedic (Dhyana)
- **Why:** Elizabeth Blackburn's Nobel research: meditators have 30% longer telomeres. Reduces cortisol, IL-6 inflammation. Buddhist monks show 25-year younger brain scans
- **Do:** 20 min minimum, focus on breath
- **Time:** 20 min

### 7. ZONE 2 CARDIO (Running/Walking)
- **Source:** Modern Science (Peter Attia), Japanese walking culture
- **Why:** Builds mitochondrial density (cellular power plants). VO2 Max is #1 predictor of longevity. Every 1 unit increase in VO2 Max = 13% lower mortality
- **Do:** 5K run daily (you already do this)
- **Time:** 30-45 min

### 8. SUNLIGHT EXPOSURE (First 30 min)
- **Source:** Ayurveda (Surya Namaskar tradition), Modern circadian science
- **Why:** Sets circadian clock, triggers vitamin D synthesis, regulates cortisol. Andrew Huberman: "Morning sunlight is the single most important thing for health"
- **Do:** 10 min outdoor light within 1 hour of waking
- **Time:** 10 min

## DAILY NUTRITION

### 9. HARA HACHI BU — Eat Until 80% Full
- **Source:** Okinawan tradition (Japan Blue Zone)
- **Why:** Okinawans live to 100+. Caloric restriction activates sirtuins (longevity genes). 20% less calories = 30% more lifespan in every species tested
- **Do:** Stop eating when slightly satisfied, not stuffed
- **Time:** 0 min (habit)

### 10. TRIPHALA — Before Bed
- **Source:** Ayurveda (Charaka Samhita — called "Rasayana" / rejuvenation)
- **Why:** Three fruits (Amla + Haritaki + Bibhitaki). Most powerful Ayurvedic anti-aging compound. Amla has 20x vitamin C of oranges. Cleanses all 7 dhatus (tissues). Modern: rich in gallic acid, antioxidant, gut microbiome optimizer
- **Do:** 1 tsp powder in warm water before bed
- **Time:** 2 min

### 11. ASHWAGANDHA (Withania Somnifera)
- **Source:** Ayurveda — called "Rasayana for all" in Charaka Samhita
- **Why:** Reduces cortisol 30%, improves testosterone, increases telomerase activity. KSM-66 form shown to reverse biological age markers. Adaptogen — balances whatever is off
- **Do:** 600mg KSM-66 daily, morning or evening
- **Time:** 1 min

### 12. GREEN TEA (Matcha preferred)
- **Source:** Chinese/Japanese tradition (Sen no Rikyu, Zen Buddhism)
- **Why:** EGCG activates AMPK (cellular cleanup). Japanese study: 5+ cups/day = 26% lower all-cause mortality. Catechins protect telomeres. L-theanine for calm focus
- **Do:** 2-3 cups daily, no sugar
- **Time:** 5 min

### 13. TURMERIC + BLACK PEPPER (Golden Milk)
- **Source:** Ayurveda, Chinese medicine
- **Why:** Curcumin is the most studied anti-aging compound. Anti-inflammatory (blocks NF-kB), neuroprotective, activates Nrf2 (antioxidant pathway). Black pepper increases absorption 2000%
- **Do:** 1 tsp turmeric + pinch black pepper in warm milk, evening
- **Time:** 5 min

### 14. INTERMITTENT FASTING (16:8 or 18:6)
- **Source:** Buddhist monk tradition (no food after noon), Ayurvedic Ekadashi fasting, Modern science (Yoshinori Ohsumi Nobel 2016)
- **Why:** Activates autophagy (cellular garbage cleanup). Removes damaged proteins, recycling old mitochondria. Ohsumi proved fasting triggers self-cleaning. Reduces biological age
- **Do:** Eat within 8-hour window (10 AM - 6 PM)
- **Time:** 0 min (just timing)

## DAILY EVENING

### 15. ABHYANGA — Self Oil Massage
- **Source:** Ayurveda (Charaka Samhita Ch.5 — "Jarahara" = destroys old age)
- **Why:** Charaka literally says "prevents aging" (jarahara). Warm sesame oil penetrates 7 layers of skin. Increases circulation, moves lymph, nourishes tissues. Reduces cortisol
- **Do:** Warm sesame oil, massage whole body 10 min before shower
- **Time:** 10 min (weekly minimum, daily ideal)

### 16. SLEEP 7-8 HOURS (Before 10 PM)
- **Source:** Ayurveda (Kapha time 6-10 PM), Matthew Walker research
- **Why:** Growth hormone peaks 10 PM - 2 AM ONLY if asleep. Deep sleep clears brain amyloid (Alzheimer's prevention). Sleep deprivation ages you 10 years on biomarkers
- **Do:** Bed by 10 PM, dark room, no screens 1hr before
- **Time:** 7-8 hrs

### 17. NASYA — Nasal Oil Application
- **Source:** Ayurveda (Ashtanga Hridayam)
- **Why:** "Nasa hi shiraso dwaram" — nose is the gateway to the brain. Lubricates nasal passages, prevents grey hair, preserves eyesight, prevents wrinkles on face
- **Do:** 2 drops warm sesame/anu oil in each nostril, morning
- **Time:** 2 min

## WEEKLY

### 18. STRENGTH TRAINING (3x/week)
- **Source:** Modern science (Peter Attia, Brad Schoenfeld)
- **Why:** Muscle is the "organ of longevity." Prevents sarcopenia. Increases insulin sensitivity, bone density, growth hormone. After 30, you lose 3-8% muscle per decade without training
- **Do:** Full body compound movements (squats, deadlifts, press, rows)
- **Time:** 45-60 min x 3/week

### 19. FOREST BATHING (Shinrin-Yoku) — Weekly
- **Source:** Japanese tradition (researched by Dr. Qing Li)
- **Why:** Trees emit phytoncides that boost NK (natural killer) cells by 50% for 7 days. Reduces cortisol, blood pressure, inflammation. 2 hours in forest = 1 week of immune boost
- **Do:** Walk in nature/park for 2+ hours weekly
- **Time:** 2 hrs/week

### 20. EKADASHI FASTING (twice monthly)
- **Source:** Vedic tradition (Vishnu Purana, Padma Purana)
- **Why:** 24-hour fast on 11th day of lunar cycle. Deepest autophagy activation (48hr peak). Gut reset. Spiritual clarity
- **Do:** Water-only fast from sunrise to next sunrise
- **Time:** 24 hrs, twice/month

### 21. QIGONG / TAI CHI (2-3x/week)
- **Source:** Chinese Taoist tradition (over 4000 years)
- **Why:** "Moving meditation" — reduces biological age by 5 years in studies. Improves balance (prevents falls — #1 cause of death in elderly), reduces inflammation, enhances immune function
- **Do:** 20 min slow flowing movements (YouTube: "Qigong for beginners")
- **Time:** 20 min x 3/week

## DAILY MICRO-HABITS

### 22. EARTHING/GROUNDING — Walk Barefoot
- **Source:** Vedic (Prithvi connection), Modern (Clint Ober research)
- **Why:** Earth's surface electrons neutralize free radicals. Reduces blood viscosity (thinner blood), inflammation markers. Atharva Veda: "Prithvi is the healer"
- **Do:** 10 min barefoot on grass/earth daily
- **Time:** 10 min

### 23. AMLA (Indian Gooseberry) — Daily
- **Source:** Ayurveda — called "Dhatri" (the nurse) in Charaka Samhita
- **Why:** Highest natural vitamin C source. Rasayana #1 in Ayurveda. Protects DNA from oxidative damage. Anti-glycation (prevents sugar-protein binding that ages skin). Reverses grey hair
- **Do:** 1 fresh amla or 1 tsp amla powder daily
- **Time:** 1 min

### 24. JAPA / MANTRA MEDITATION
- **Source:** Vedic (Rig Veda Gayatri Mantra), Buddhist chanting
- **Why:** Vibration of "Om" activates vagus nerve at 7.83Hz (Earth's Schumann resonance). Reduces amygdala reactivity. Buddhist monks who chant daily show 15-year younger brains on MRI
- **Do:** 108 repetitions of chosen mantra (Gayatri, Om Namah Shivaya, or simple Om)
- **Time:** 10 min

### 25. IKIGAI — Purpose Practice
- **Source:** Japanese (Okinawa Blue Zone)
- **Why:** Okinawans with strong ikigai live 7 years longer. Purpose reduces cortisol, inflammation, cardiovascular risk. "He who has a WHY can bear almost any HOW" — Nietzsche
- **Do:** Write your purpose every morning. Review nightly
- **FirstLight IS your ikigai**
- **Time:** 2 min
