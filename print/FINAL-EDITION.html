PART 1

The Research — What 20 Years Actually Did

1. Your brain isn’t broken. It adapted.

  • Reward downregulation. Supernormal stimulation turned the reward system down. Ordinary life — workouts, food, people — feels gray. This is the numbness. It is reversible.
  • The craving circuit is over-trained; the brake is under-trained. Brain-imaging on porn addiction shows hyperactive salience networks and inhibited control regions.
  • Wanting ≠ liking. Your brain learned to want porn intensely while liking it less. “I still want it, and it makes me numb” is the signature of a conditioned cue→craving loop — not weakness.
  • Attentional bias. Attention is captured by sexual cues before any “choice.” The loop fires in seconds from cues: the empty room, the late hour, the phone in hand, boredom.
  • The anhedonia is why workouts died. Normal rewards stopped registering. Remove the stimulus, reintroduce real rewards, and the baseline returns.

2. Why every oath failed — proven, not motivational

The Abstinence Violation Effect (Marlatt & Gordon): breaking an all-or-nothing promise triggers internal, stable, global attributions — “I’m weak, I always fail, everything is ruined.” That converts one slip into full collapse. The oath is the machinery that manufactures the collapse.

THE CYCLE
Oath → white-knuckle → slip → shame storm (AVE) → “I’m hopeless” → binge → new oath

Shame vs self-compassion — the data is one-sided. Shame predicts worse outcomes and deeper relapse; self-compassion is a protective factor that predicts sustained recovery. Self-compassion is not letting yourself off — it is the attitude that survives a slip.

What is NOT supported: NoFap as identity (more forum engagement correlated with worse symptoms); “dopamine detox” as protocol; testosterone myths. What survives from that world: removing the stimulus for weeks does help reset reward sensitivity. Keep the mechanism, drop the cult.

The Mind — What No Machine Watches

The chain, and where the rule cuts it

SEXUAL THOUGHT → urge → screen → porn → escalation → shame → stronger thought

Thoughts cannot be banned — and ordering them away makes them stronger (ironic rebound). The device rule doesn’t fight the thought; it cuts the chain at the screen. With the door gone, a thought rises, finds no door, and dies in minutes. That is the entire mechanism.

The Urge Drill — memorise

  1. NAME IT: “This is the wave. Dopamine asking. It peaks in 15–30 minutes and dies. I don’t have to obey it.”
  2. SURF IT: don’t fight, don’t obey — observe. Locate it in the body. Breathe through the peak.
  3. MOVE: 20 pushups. Cold water on the face. Walk out of the room. Motion ends the wave.
  4. FIRE THE IF-THEN: the pre-written handler. No decision, no negotiation.

Pre-written handlers: IF urge at desk → THEN close everything, 20 pushups, 5-min walk · IF urge after 9 pm → THEN keys, 10-min drive, call the witness from the car · IF urge + boredom → THEN call or walk, never a screen · IF lying in bed awake and the mind spins → THEN stand up NOW, cold water, pushups, book until sleepy.

The Bed Law

Bed = sleep only. Nothing else. Awake 20 minutes → get up. The bed is your strongest cue — only enter it sleepy. The 11 pm play: say “this is the wave” → stand up now → cold water → pushups → book in the living room → return only when eyes are heavy. It weakens fast (cue extinction): by week 4, 11 pm is boring-sleepy, not dangerous.

Masturbation — the honest line

Biology is not the enemy. The enemy is the chain. Never with a screen · not in bed during the first 30 days · zero shame after · never miss twice. The danger isn’t the release — it’s the next link toward a screen. Break the chain at the first link.

The Slip Protocol — pre-written, because slips will come

  1. No oath. No drama. The clock keeps running. Only quitting is failure.
  2. 3-line log: time, mood, trigger. Data, not confession.
  3. Physically leave. Shower. Walk.
  4. Back on the run the same day.
  5. Tell the witness tonight: “Slipped. Back on it.”
  6. Never miss twice. The first slip is a lesson. The second is the relapse.
