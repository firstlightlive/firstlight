NOT TODAY

THE 60-DAY PLAN · NO SCREENS IN THE HOUSE · ONE DECISION A DAY

This is a contract, not a vow. Vows are made to yourself at midnight and broken at midnight. Contracts are made to another person in daylight and enforced by architecture. For 20 years the war was hourly — a thousand decisions in the dark. This plan replaces all of them with one decision a day, made at the door, when you are strong:

“IS THIS A SCREEN? THEN IT DOES NOT CROSS THIS DOOR.”

The 3 Laws

1 · NO VOWS. ONLY TODAY. No “never again,” no streak worship. A slip is data, not death. Only quitting is failure. Every morning: not today.

2 · NEVER MISS TWICE. One slip is a data point. Two in a row is a collapse. The gap between slip #1 and slip #2 is where this war is won.

3 · NO SECRECY. One human knows everything, every day. The nightly check-in is not optional — not after a good day, not after a slip. Especially after a slip.

The Contract

1. For 60 days, no screen device — phone, laptop, tablet, anything with a browser — crosses the door of my house. Day 1 is the first full day after signing.

2. A slip is not a reset. If a device crosses the door, I remove it, I text my witness that night, and the clock keeps running. Only quitting is failure.

3. The only devices allowed inside: a dumb phone (calls + texts only), a speaker/radio, an alarm clock. That is the complete list.

4. My smartphone and laptop live at the office — locker or desk — not in my car, not in my house.

5. Every night, one line to my witness: “Day __, clean” or “Slipped, back on it.”

6. On Day 61, the re-entry rules (page 3) become permanent house law.

SIGNED (me) — in daylight, not at midnight DATE
WITNESSED BY — holds my keys, gets one line every night DATE

WITNESS’S JOB: hold the phone passcodes/PIN. Receive one line every night. If the check-in stops for 48 hours, you call. That is the entire job.

  • Dinner is cooked, ordered, or carried in before the door closes behind you.
  • You are a man who plans his evening in the afternoon. That habit is part of the cure.

The Urge Drill — Memorize This

Urges are waves: they rise, peak in 15–30 minutes, and crash on their own — whether you act or not. Don’t fight them. Watch them pass.

  1. NAME IT: “This is the urge. Dopamine asking. It peaks in minutes and dies. I don’t have to obey it.”
  2. SURF IT: don’t fight, don’t obey — observe. Locate it in the body. Breathe through the peak. Suppression makes it stronger; watching kills it.
  3. MOVE: 20 pushups. Cold water on the face. Walk out of the room. Motion ends the wave.
  4. FIRE THE IF-THEN: the pre-written handler below. No decision. No negotiation.

Pre-Written IF-THEN Plans (tape on desk, fridge, car dashboard)

IF urge at the desk → THEN close everything, 20 pushups, 5-minute walk outside.
IF urge after 9 pm → THEN keys in hand, 10-minute drive, call your witness from the car.
IF urge from stress or boredom → THEN call or walk — never touch a screen.
IF hand reaches for any screen after 9 pm → THEN stand up and leave the room. Instantly.
IF weekend night alone and restless → THEN gym, or a long drive with an audiobook. Never the couch.
IF lying in bed awake and the mind starts spinning → THEN stand up NOW. Cold water, 20 pushups, book until sleepy. Bed = sleep only.

The Slip Protocol — Pre-Written, Because Slips Will Come

A slip is a data point, not a verdict. The relapse only begins if you add shame, secrecy, or a second slip on top of it.

  1. NO OATH. NO DRAMA. The 60-day clock keeps running. Only quitting is failure.
  2. 3-LINE LOG: time, mood, what happened 10 minutes before. Data, not confession.
  3. PHYSICALLY LEAVE. Shower. Walk. Move the body out of the scene.
  4. BACK ON THE RUN THE SAME DAY.
  5. TELL THE WITNESS TONIGHT: “Slipped. Back on it.”
  6. NEVER MISS TWICE. The first slip is a lesson. The second is the relapse. Keep them apart.

The Fuel Line — You Live Alone, So Build Humans In

  • Daily: one phone call, 10 minutes minimum, to a real human. Scheduled. A voice is the antidote to a screen.
  • Weekly: one in-person meeting — dinner, chai, a drive. Non-negotiable.
  • One group: gym class, running club, badminton, trek group. You don’t need friends — you need scheduled humans.

The Penal Code — Pre-Agreed, Paid in Distance, Never in Shame

2 · Device used inside the house → 200 km cycle.
3 · Device in bedroom / after 9:30 → 300 km cycle.

EXCEPTIONS (forced only, never chosen): illness · emergency · lockdown · festival closure · critical work. Protocol: witness told, device in bag, daylight-only, ends when the force ends. Faked exception = lying = 400 km cycle.

The Closing Truth — Read This When It Gets Hard

For 20 years you fought with oaths, and oaths demand strength at midnight — alone, in the dark, against a brain trained for two decades to want this. You were not weak. You were outmatched by the arena. This plan changes the arena. One decision a day, in daylight, at a door: is this a screen? Then it stays outside. You can win one decision.

The first week will feel rough — restless, bored, sure this is pointless. That feeling is 20 years of wiring coming loose. It passes. When a slip comes: no oath, three lines, out of the room, back the same day, one honest text. Never miss twice.

“I do not need to win 60 days.
I need to win today.
Tomorrow, I will win tomorrow.
NOT TODAY.”
