║  THE FINAL RULE — FOR LIFE                    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════╝

NOT TODAY.

1. THE DOOR
   Screens never cross my door. Not at home — ever.
   In a hotel, every screen goes OFF from room-entry
   to morning. My bed, wherever it is, is for sleep only.

2. THE LINE
   Dumb phone always on. Every night, one line to my
   witness: "Day __, clean."

3. THE THREE LAWS
   No secrets. No vows. Never miss twice.

4. THE DAYLIGHT
   All work and all screens live in daylight, outside,
   among people — office, cafe, cabin, road.

5. THE WAVE
   An urge is a wave. Name it, watch it, move.
   It dies in 20 minutes.

6. THE PRICE (if a screen crosses the door)
   Connected .... 50 km cycle/hour (day) · 100 km/h (night)
                  rounded up, min 1 hour, starts from zero
   Used in house ............... 200 km cycle
   Bedroom / after 9:30 ........ 300 km cycle
   Lied / hid / guard off ...... 400 km cycle + 30 days no exceptions
   Two slips in 7 days ......... 500 km cycle
   Unpaid after 48h ............ DOUBLES
   Pay: cycle 1 = walk 1 = run 1 = swim 1 km = 4
   Proof to the witness.

7. THE ONLY DOORS OUT
   Illness · emergency · lockdown · festival · critical work —
   declared to the witness FIRST, daylight, logged,
   expires when the force ends.
   A declared trip is a reward, not a break.

8. THE SLIP
   No oath. Three-line log. Out of the room.
   Back the same day. Tell the witness tonight.
   Never miss twice.

9. NEVER PUNISHED WITH
   Shame. A reset clock. New oaths. Public posting.

10. THE LAST LINE
    Only quitting is failure. The clock never resets.
    NOT TODAY.
