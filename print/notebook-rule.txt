NOT TODAY.

1. Screens never cross my door.
   Not at home. Not in a hotel room. Bed = sleep only.
2. Dumb phone always on.
   One line to the witness every night: "Day __, clean."
3. No secrets. No vows. Never miss twice.
4. All work and screens: daylight, outside, among people.
5. Urge = a wave. Watch it pass. Move. It dies in 20 minutes.

── PUNISHMENT ──────────────────────────────
Screen crossed the door ............ 100 km cycle
Used it in the house ............... 200 km cycle
Bedroom, or after 9:30 ............. 300 km cycle
Lied / hid / guard off ............. 400 km cycle + 30 days no exceptions
Two slips in 7 days ................ 500 km cycle
Unpaid after 48 hours .............. DOUBLES

Pay with: cycle (2 km = 1) · walk (1 = 1) · run (1 = 1) · swim (1 km = 4)
Proof to the witness. Deadline 48h.
Never punished with: shame, resetting the clock, new oaths.

Only quitting is failure. The clock never resets. NOT TODAY.
