THE RULE
No screen ever enters my house — not the laptop, not the
phone, not any device — and in a hotel, every screen is
switched OFF the moment I enter the room.

THE PUNISHMENT
1. Connected for an hour: 50 km cycle by day, 100 km cycle
   by night — any part of an hour is a full hour.
   (CONNECTED = device joined the home WiFi. Price = hours ×
   rate. 2 daytime hours = 100 km. 10 minutes = still 50 km.)
2. Used inside the house: 200 km cycle. Bedroom or after
   9:30: 300 km cycle.
   (USED = you actually opened/used it — flat price, no matter
   how long. Bedroom or after 9:30 = 300 km flat.)
   If more than one applies: the HEAVIEST price wins, never stacked.
3. Lied or hid it: 400 km cycle and 30 days with no exceptions.
4. Two slips in seven days: 500 km cycle. Unpaid within 48
   hours: the debt doubles.
5. Paid by cycle, walk, or run — kilometre for kilometre —
   swim counts four times, proof to the witness.
