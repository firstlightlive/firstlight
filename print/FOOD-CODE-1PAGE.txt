THE RULE
No frying. No sugar. No alcohol. No smoking.
No cold drinks. No junk.

Sugar is TRAINING FUEL — on the ride or the run
it is medicine. Off it, it's a violation.
No ice cream, ever.

THE EXCEPTION (only one)
Training over 90 min: gels / dates / banana — free.
Race day: free.

THE PRICE
Break any line → 50 km ride · posted.

THE PRICE — IN ANY PAYMENT
ride 50 km
walk 25 km     (walk counts double)
run 25 km
swim 5 km
gym / boxing 5 h
Unpaid after 48 hours → doubles.
Vomiting does NOT erase it — the walk is still owed.
Proof to the witness.

no Zomato, no Swiggy, no ice-cream runs. The ORDER

Break → 60 km ride (or 30 km walk — counts double).

Nothing marked by 11:59 PM =
ALL rules violated.
