July 2026, from my own tracked data: bedtimes 00:19 — 02:31 AM. That same month: running collapsed

## THE SEVEN RULES

### RULE 1 — THE PHONE LIVES IN THE HALL. IT TRAVELS NOWHERE.
One drawer, in the hall. That is the phone's entire territory on Earth.
It does not enter the bedroom, the study, the office room, the kitchen, the car, or a pocket.
On office days it stays HOME in its drawer — powered on, on WiFi, silently syncing my health data
all day (which keeps the streak-judge fed). I don't carry it because I don't carry it. Period.

### RULE 2 — IT SLEEPS 7:00 PM → 9:00 AM. POWERED OFF.
Not silent. Not face down. POWER OFF at 7:00 PM. A powered phone negotiates; a dead phone is
furniture. It wakes at 9:00 AM into a day that is already won — meditation, 90 minutes of deep
study, the swim, the gym, study 2 all finished by 8:50. It wakes as a tool, at market open.

### RULE 3 — ONE TOUCH A DAY: 6:40–6:55 PM. AND ANSWERED CALLS. NOTHING ELSE.
The single sanctioned touch: standing in the hall, 6:40–6:55 PM — log the day in the system,
clear OTP backlog, send the cook tomorrow's menu, then POWER OFF at 7:00.
The only other legal contact: ANSWERING an incoming call, in the hall, and putting it back.
Absolute rules die from undefined exceptions. This rule has exactly two, both defined. It will hold.

### RULE 4 — THE WATCH IS THE WORLD'S DOOR.
Apple Watch cellular eSIM — CONFIRMED: shares my number, rings even with the phone off.
Anyone who calls reaches my wrist in seconds, day or night. Hardly anyone calls — the cost is zero.
The watch carries: calls, favourites' messages, the 3:30 alarm, workout tracking. Nothing else.
**Watch charging is a ritual, not a habit: every day during the 7:15 breakfast + shower.**
A dead watch = unreachable. Its battery is now covenant-grade.

### RULE 5 — NO FEEDS LIVE ON THE PHONE. EVER.
Instagram: DELETED — the system posts my daily proof automatically; I never needed the app.
YouTube, Reels, news apps: DELETED. Screen Time: Downtime 7 PM–9 AM, 15-min social limits,
passcode on paper in the locked room. Grayscale on.
What remains is a landline with a camera.

### RULE 6 — THE DESKTOP DOES THE PHONE'S OLD JOBS.
WhatsApp Web on the desk (cook messages) · broker/bank TOTP in a DESKTOP authenticator —
MANDATORY, there is no morning phone window · Zepto/BigBasket/payments via web ·
₹200 + card in the scooty pouch. The phone keeps no job that could summon me to the hall.

### RULE 7 — WITNESSED EVERY SUNDAY. PRICED EVERY BREACH.
Weekly Review, in pen: Screen Time daily average · pickups · phone-off held ___/7 · breaches paid.
The number does not lie, and a falling number is a streak like any other.
