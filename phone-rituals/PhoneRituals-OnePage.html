THE RULES
1 · HOME ZONE — drawer only: inside the house the phone is ON in exactly one place: the hall drawer. Use there is UNLIMITED — standing, any OTP, authenticator, code, order, 9:00–7:00. It never leaves the drawer space ON; moving within the house = powered OFF.
2 · WORK ZONE — bag or car, never the desk: at the office the phone waits in the bag or the car, silent. The desk is for the market, not the phone.
3 · WORLD ZONE — tool in hand: road, mall, hospital, parking, party, travel — carry it, PAY with it (UPI), navigate (in the car: propped in a holder or seat — never handled while driving), book, click, call. STANDING and MOVING use only: never on a couch, never at a dining table, never idle-sitting with it.
4 · THE NIGHT: 6:40–6:55 PM at the drawer — log the day · sync Strava/HAE · cook's menu → POWER OFF at 7:00, everywhere on Earth (traveling: the hotel safe = the drawer). ON again 9:00 AM. Between those hours it is furniture.
5 · THE WATCH IS THE WORLD'S DOOR: eSIM shares my number — rings even with the phone off (confirmed). Calls, favourites, the 3:30 alarm, workouts, gym music via AirPods. Charged daily at breakfast — covenant-grade. Door OTPs (Urban Company, deliveries) are read off the wrist — SMS mirrors there.
6 · NO SOCIAL MEDIA LIVES ON ANY PHONE I OWN. EVER. Instagram, YouTube, every feed — never installed, on any phone (the system posts my proof automatically; email confirms it). Downtime 7 PM–9 AM · grayscale · Screen Time passcode locked away. THE INSTALL TAX — the only legal social session: go to a COFFEE SHOP or CO-WORKING SPACE → install → do the task → DELETE before leaving the building. Never installed at home, at the office, or "just for tonight". THE LAPTOP TOO: YouTube + Instagram blocked in the browser (hosts file). Listening happens on the Apple Watch + AirPods (its battery is the natural time cap) or the iPod — no screen ever plays a feed. What remains on the phone is a code-machine with a camera.
7 · ORDER OF TOOLS — watch → desktop → drawer: wrist first (codes, calls, gym music) · desktop for orders, bookings, WhatsApp Web, broker TOTP · the drawer for app-only jobs (Snabbit check-in). ₹200 + card in the scooty.

WHY — THE CHAIN THIS PROTECTS
Everything I want — the races, the two missions, the market — is manufactured in one place: the morning, 3:30 to 9:00. The morning exists only if the night ends at 8:45. So the whole system stands on one foundation: the night.

The phone is the only object in my life engineered, deliberately, by thousands of people, to take the night. My own data proves it worked: July 2026 — bedtimes 00:19–02:31 → sleep broke → training collapsed → the streak died. Fifteen years, fifty attempts, same ending, and every collapse began after dark with a screen.

Willpower lost all fifty times. So this book does not use willpower — it uses geography. A phone that is only ever ON inside a drawer cannot take a night. The drawer decides, so I don't have to. Guard the night, and the mornings build everything else.
