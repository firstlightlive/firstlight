# ANUPAM - MASTERY SKILL TRACKER
### Track Daily. Review Weekly. Score Monthly.
### Covers: Social Intelligence | Communication | Mental Performance | Brand & Creativity | Relationships | Spiritual Wisdom

## DAILY MASTERY ITEMS (25 Items)
*Check each item every day. Score = items completed / 25. Target: 80%+ daily.*

---

### A. PEOPLE READING & SOCIAL INTELLIGENCE (5 items)
*Source: 7 Pillars of Social Intelligence, Human Psychology, Body Language, Pattern Recognition*

| # | Daily Action | Domain | How to Track |
|---|-------------|--------|-------------|
| 1 | **Read 1 person's baseline** - Pick one person you interact with. Observe their normal body language, speech pace, eye contact, energy. Note deviations from baseline. | Pattern Recognition | Name + 1 observation written down |
| 2 | **Feet First check** - In every important conversation, notice where their feet point. Toward you = engaged. Toward exit = wants to leave. Log 1 observation. | Body Language | 1 logged observation |
| 3 | **Room Read within 60 seconds** - When entering any group setting, do a 5-point scan: Who has power? Who is comfortable? Who is anxious? What are the alliances? What is the energy? | Social Dynamics | Done once per group entry |
| 4 | **Make 3 predictions** - At morning planning, predict 3 things about people/situations you'll encounter today. Check accuracy at night. | Pattern Recognition | 3 predictions written + accuracy scored at night |
| 5 | **Spot 3 cognitive biases** - In yourself or others. Name the bias specifically (confirmation bias, anchoring, halo effect, etc.) | Human Psychology | 3 biases named + context |

---

### B. COMMUNICATION & INFLUENCE (5 items)
*Source: Communication Mastery Bible, Persuasion & Influence, Active Listening*

| # | Daily Action | Domain | How to Track |
|---|-------------|--------|-------------|
| 6 | **Learn 5 new vocabulary words** - Use the Norman Lewis concept-cluster method. Learn the idea, then the word. Use each in 3 contexts within 24 hours (written, spoken, internal). | Vocabulary Power | 5 words written with roots + usage |
| 7 | **3-Second Listening Rule** - In every important conversation, wait 3 full seconds after the other person finishes before responding. Do this minimum 3 times today. | Active Listening | 3 conversations where you held the pause |
| 8 | **Give value to 3 people (Reciprocity)** - Help, useful information, or genuine compliment. No expectation of return. Pure investment. | Persuasion & Influence | 3 names + what you gave |
| 9 | **Practice 1 storytelling framework** - Take any event from your day and restructure it using: Hook > Tension > Resolution. Tell it to someone or write it down. | Storytelling | 1 story structured and delivered/written |
| 10 | **Emotional Labeling 3x** - Use "It seems like..." or "It sounds like you're feeling..." to label one person's emotion in 3 separate interactions. (Chris Voss technique) | Persuasion & Negotiation | 3 labeling attempts + response noted |

### C. MENTAL PERFORMANCE & DEEP WORK (5 items)
*Source: Domain 5 Mental Performance, Cal Newport, Stoic Philosophy*

| # | Daily Action | Domain | How to Track |
|---|-------------|--------|-------------|
| 11 | **Stoic Preparation (5 min morning)** - What might challenge me today? Pre-decide your emotional response. Practice Dichotomy of Control. Write it down. | Emotional Regulation | Written in journal |
| 12 | **Emotional Check-in (1-10 score)** - Rate your emotional state. Name the specific emotions. Do a body scan for tension. Do this morning + post-lunch + evening = 3x/day. | Emotional Intelligence | 3 scores + named emotions logged |
| 13 | **Deep Work sessions completed** - Count how many 90-min focused blocks you completed. Phone in bag, one task, physical timer. No exceptions. Target: minimum 3 sessions. | Focus & Flow | Number logged (target: 3+) |
| 14 | **Apply 1 mental model deliberately** - Use one mental model in a real decision today: Inversion, Second-Order Thinking, Occam's Razor, First Principles, Circle of Competence, etc. Name which one and how you applied it. | Cognitive Enhancement | Model named + application described |
| 15 | **6-Second Rule** - Before any emotional reaction, pause for 6 seconds. Breathe. Then respond. Track how many times you caught yourself and paused vs. reacted. | Emotional Regulation | Pauses caught / reactions (ratio) |

### D. BRAND, CREATIVITY & CAREER (5 items)
*Source: Brand Building Encyclopedia, 25 Rules for Life, First Light Declaration*

| # | Daily Action | Domain | How to Track |
|---|-------------|--------|-------------|
| 16 | **15 min craft practice** - Work on your ONE skill. Every single day. Not to show off, not for money. Because mastery demands daily reps. Even on the worst days. (Rule 5: Become So Good They Cannot Ignore You) | Craft Mastery | 15 min minimum logged |
| 17 | **1 piece of content created or refined** - Write, record, design, or refine one piece of brand content. Could be a post, a script, a framework, a visual. Build your brand daily. | Personal Brand | 1 item created/refined |
| 18 | **Actions > Words audit** - Did you complain today? Did you announce plans instead of executing? Did you let results speak? Score yourself honestly. (Rule 10: Let Your Actions Speak) | Character & Discipline | Y/N self-audit with note |
| 19 | **"Am I doing this as well as I can right now?"** - Ask this once per hour during work. Make 1 small adjustment each time. Excellence in small things = excellence in big things. (Rule 13: Bring Your Best to Every Task) | Excellence | Times asked + adjustments made |
| 20 | **Write your WHY statement** - Every morning, write or recite: "I exist to ___ so that ___." Refine it by 1% each day until it is undeniable. (Simon Sinek - Start With Why) | Purpose & Brand | Written/recited. Any refinement noted. |

### E. RELATIONSHIPS, WISDOM & INNER GAME (5 items)
*Source: Dating Mastery, Osho, 25 Rules for Life, First Light Declaration*

| # | Daily Action | Domain | How to Track |
|---|-------------|--------|-------------|
| 21 | **Make 1 person feel genuinely seen** - Use their name. Remember something about them. Acknowledge them before the agenda. Ask "How can I make this person feel they matter today?" (Rule 6 + Rule 19: Truly Listen) | Relationships & Social | Name + what you did |
| 22 | **Start 1 hard conversation with a story, not accusation** - "I noticed something. Can I share it?" Not "You always..." Listen to their side completely before giving yours. (Rule 9) | Difficult Conversations | 1 conversation handled this way (or N/A) |
| 23 | **Ho'oponopono or Forgiveness practice (5 min)** - Direct 4 phrases at anyone you hold resentment toward, including yourself: I love you. I'm sorry. Please forgive me. Thank you. | Spiritual / Inner Game | 5 min completed. Who directed at. |
| 24 | **Invest in 1 relationship with zero expectation** - Do one thing for someone before you need anything from them. Help, share info, open a door. Relationships are your real salary. (Rule 2: Invest in People Before You Need Them) | Social Capital | Name + what you invested |
| 25 | **Accept the worst case, then act** - Name 1 thing you are afraid to start or avoiding. Say: "I accept this might fail. I am doing it anyway." Then take 1 action toward it. (Rule 12 + Rule 25) | Courage & Action | Fear named + action taken |

## WEEKLY MASTERY ITEMS (7 Items)
*Complete once per week. Best done on Sunday during your PERMA audit.*

| # | Weekly Action | Domain | How to Track |
|---|-------------|--------|-------------|
| W1 | **Body Language Log Review** - Review all body language observations from the week. What patterns emerged? What did you miss? Write 3 insights. | Body Language & Pattern Recognition | 3 insights written |
| W2 | **Social Dynamics Debrief** - Review all social interactions. Where did you command respect? Where did you lose frame? What red flags did you spot? What dynamics did you observe? | Social Dynamics | Written debrief |
| W3 | **Prediction Accuracy Score** - Tally all 21 daily predictions (3/day x 7). What % were accurate? Where were you wrong? What does the pattern tell you about your blind spots? | Pattern Recognition | Score: __/21 correct. Blind spots noted. |
| W4 | **Communication Skill Focus** - Pick 1 chapter from Communication Mastery Bible. Do the exercises. Practice the frameworks. Rotate weekly: Vocabulary > Fluency > Storytelling > Persuasion > Professional > Written > Listening > Body Language > Difficult Conversations > Public Speaking > Humor | Communication | Chapter + exercises completed |
| W5 | **Brand Strategy Review** - Review your brand positioning, one-liner, and WHY statement. Is your messaging clear? Are actions aligned with your brand? Refine 1 element. | Brand Building | Element refined + what changed |
| W6 | **Mental Model Collection** - Study 1 new mental model deeply. Write it in your own words. Find 3 real-life applications. Add it to your mental model inventory. | Cognitive Enhancement | Model name + 3 applications written |
| W7 | **Relationship Audit (PERMA)** - Score your week: P(ositive emotions), E(ngagement), R(elationships), M(eaning), A(ccomplishment). Each 1-10. Total /50. Who did you invest in? Who invested in you? Who do you need to reach out to? | Relationships & Wellbeing | PERMA score __/50. 3 action items for next week. |
