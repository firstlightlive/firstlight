### Compiled 2026-08-22 · for Anupam Kumar · sources: meta-analyses, RCTs, ISSN/ACSM/IOC position stands, 2023–2026

Context assumed: male ~40, lacto-vegetarian Indian diet, Ironman-distance training (fasted 4:35–7:00 AM block),
eating window ~7 AM–6 PM, deep work 8:15 AM–5:30 PM, lights out 8:45–10 PM.

## PART 1 — BUGS IN THE CURRENT SHEET (fix regardless of nutrition)

1. **Lights-out contradiction.** Evening block ends `8:45 LIGHTS OUT ★`, but the ANCHORS box says
   "Sleep: 5.5h — lights out ~10 PM → wake 3:30 AM". 8:45 PM → 3:30 AM is actually **6h45m** (good!).
   Pick one truth and update the anchor text — the 5.5h figure undersells what the schedule actually delivers.
2. **Lunch block times are out of order.** `2:00 Lunch` is listed before `1:50 Shata Pada (after lunch)` and
   `1:55 warm water`. Lunch should read **1:30**, then 1:50/1:55 make sense.
3. **Dinner does not exist on the sheet.** "Last solid meal before 6 PM" is a Food-Code rule with no checklist
   item — the single most important meal of the recovery day is unlogged. Add an explicit **5:30 DINNER** item.
4. **5.5-hour food gap** between breakfast (8:10) and lunch (~1:30–2:00) with deep work in the middle.
   Add a ~10:25 fruit item (before the no-electronics deep-study block).
5. **Hydration is only tracked to noon** (750 ml). No full-day target exists. Add an evening checkpoint.
6. Minor: "AT OFFICE — BREAKFAST 7:45" vs "Leave for office 11:50" — label one of them (home desk vs office).

## PART 2 — THE 10 EVIDENCE-BACKED ADDITIONS (priority order)

### 1. Creatine 5 g EVERY day, not just pre-workout — the #1 cheapest win
- Vegetarians have 20–30% lower muscle/brain creatine stores and show the largest cognitive response
  (memory, attention, processing speed — 2024 Frontiers meta-analysis, 16 RCTs; Prokopidis 2023 vegetarian subgroup).
- Timing is irrelevant; **daily saturation** is everything. Rest days included. Keep the 4:28 AM slot, just never skip.

### 2. B12 supplement + blood panel — the biggest vegetarian gap
- Unsupplemented lacto-vegetarians: ~68–77% functionally low B12 (elevated MMA). Deficiency measurably
  impairs cognition and endurance (RBC production).
- **Do:** 250–500 µg/day cyanocobalamin (or 1000 µg 2–3×/wk).
- **Test (every 6–12 months):** B12 + MMA, ferritin (+Hb, transferrin sat), 25(OH)D, HbA1c, testosterone, T3,
  LFTs (cheap insurance given the multi-herb stack). Iron: supplement ONLY if ferritin <50 ng/mL —
  then 60–100 mg elemental on **alternate mornings** with vitamin C, after the run, never near tea.

### 3. Close the ~50–80 g/day protein gap
- Target **1.8–2.0 g/kg/day** (concurrent endurance + strength at ~40 = top of ISSN range) in 4 feedings of
  ~0.4 g/kg, each hitting ~2.5–3 g leucine. Dal-roti-sabzi alone delivers ~45–60 g — roughly half of need.
- **Do:** whey (or soy) in the 7:05 AM post-workout meal · paneer/dahi at lunch · upgrade the evening
  "sprout mix" to **sprouts + sattu-milk or paneer (30–40 g protein)** at the 5:30 dinner — this doubles as the
  evidence-backed pre-sleep protein dose the day currently lacks.

### 4. Stop fasting the hard sessions (RED-S protection)
- Fasted training is fine for genuine Zone-2 ≤90 min. His 4:35–7:00 block is ~2.5 h; meta-analyses show fasted
  training buys **zero performance**, and chronic low energy availability (IOC REDs 2023) presents in men as
  low testosterone/libido, low T3, bone stress injuries, recurring illness.
- **Do:** 4:15 AM — **2–3 dates or 1 banana** (Nieman RCT: banana = sports drink) before quality/long days;
  30–60+ g CHO/h during anything >90 min; **punishment rides (30–100 km) must always be fueled**.
- **Do:** 500–750 ml water + ~400–500 mg sodium on waking (salt + nimbu water) — he starts training after an
  8-h overnight fast in Indian humidity. Monthly pre/post-workout weigh-in to calibrate sweat rate.

### 5. Caffeine curfew: last caffeinated cup by ~2 PM
- Gardiner 2023 meta-analysis (Sleep Med Rev): ~107 mg caffeine needs ≥8.8 h before bed. The 3:30 PM green tea
  sits 5–6.5 h before an 8:45 PM lights-out — borderline harmful to the scarcest asset (deep sleep).
- **Do:** 3:30 PM slot becomes **decaf green tea or plain L-theanine 100–200 mg** (keeps the calm-focus ritual).
  8:05 AM coffee is perfectly timed — and keep it ≥1 h after breakfast (tannins cut non-heme iron absorption 60–90%).

### 6. Beat the 2–4 PM fog with meal architecture, not stimulants
- Post-lunch dip is real (worst 1.5–3 h after lunch). Food-order trials: **salad + dal/paneer first, roti/rice last**
  cuts the glucose spike ~30–40%. Swap white rice → millets (ragi/jowar/bajra) or brown rice at lunch.
  Hydration: 150–250 ml/hour through the workday (~2.5–3 L/day total); >1–2% body-mass water loss measurably
  impairs attention (MSSE 2018 meta-analysis).

### 7. Fix broken supplement logic
- **L-carnitine — cut (or move).** Muscle uptake requires ~80 g carbs (insulin); fasted at 4:28 AM with water it
  never reaches muscle (Wall 2011, J Physiol). Either take with the post-workout carb meal or drop it.
- **Chyawanprash — move to breakfast.** 1 tsp = ~6–8 g sugar at 3:38 AM; it breaks the overnight fast for the
  whole training block and its immunity evidence is weak. (This also makes the "16:8" real again — currently
  chyawanprash + collagen + 7:52 PM milk stretch the true intake window to ~16 h.)
- **Collagen — shift earlier + add real vitamin C.** Shaw & Baar protocol: 15 g + ~50 mg vit C, 30–60 min before
  tendon loading. Take at ~3:40 AM (with fenugreek water) and pair with **amla** — a lemon squeeze is only
  10–20 mg vit C. Verify dose is actually 15 g.
- **Omega-3 — check the label.** Attention benefits appear at **1000–2000 mg EPA+DHA/day** (2025 Sci Rep
  dose–response meta-analysis); most vegetarian capsules carry 200–300 mg. Switch to algal EPA+DHA if short.
- **D3+K2 with breakfast is correct** (needs some fat; "largest meal" is a myth). Test 25(OH)D annually.

### 8. Daily anthocyanin serving (the one true "brain food" addition)
- Flavonoid/berry RCTs: memory + executive-function gains at ≥50–100 g berries/day equivalents.
- India-practical rotation: **jamun (in season), pomegranate, black grapes, frozen blueberries, fresh amla,
  2 squares 85% dark chocolate**. Slot: the new ~10:25 AM fruit item or with 3:30 PM almonds.

### 9. Sleep-supporting foods that actually have data
- Already correct: glycine 3 g (Yamadera RCT: faster onset, better quality) + magnesium (make sure it's
  **glycinate**, ≤350 mg elemental) + evening ashwagandha.
- Worth adding: **2 kiwis at the 6 PM meal** (RCT: −35% sleep latency) — fits as dinner dessert;
  **tart cherry** only around race weeks / punishment-ride recovery (chronic daily antioxidant loading may blunt
  training adaptation). Warm milk = fine as ritual/ashwagandha vehicle, not pharmacology.

### 10. Herbal stack hygiene (safety, not superstition)
- **Shilajit:** demand a batch-specific ICP-MS certificate (Pb/As/Cd/Hg/Tl, independent lab, <1 yr old).
  "Purified" has no legal meaning; 2024–2025 tests found heavy metals incl. thallium. **No COA = stop.**
  Its claimed benefits overlap creatine + ashwagandha with far worse evidence.
- **Ashwagandha:** India banned **leaf** in supplements (Ayush + FSSAI, Apr 2026) — verify label says
  **root-only extract** (KSM-66-type), 300–600 mg. **Cycle 8–12 wk on / 2–4 wk off**; rare hepatotoxicity
  reports (LiverTox grade C) — stop all herbals + get LFTs if dark urine/itching/jaundice.
- **Triphala:** convert daily-forever → intermittent (chronic laxative dependency, CYP450 interactions).
- **Raw sprout mix at night:** blanch/steam briefly — raw legume sprouts are a real Salmonella/E. coli vector.
- Four concurrent daily herbals (ashwagandha + shilajit + triphala + chyawanprash) = unnecessary hepatic load.

## PART 3 — WHAT *NOT* TO ADD (evidence says skip)
- **Lion's mane** — acute RCT null; the positive 8-wk trial is an unreviewed preprint. Hype > data.
- **Rhodiola** — positive trials all high risk of bias.
- **Probiotic pills for cognition** — meta-analysis null (dahi/chaas as *food* still excellent — keep).
- **Cocoa-extract pills** — COSMOS (n≈21k) null overall.
- **Optional single herbal if wanted:** Bacopa (Brahmi) 300–450 mg/day standardized ≥50% bacosides —
  best-evidenced Ayurvedic nootropic (meta-analysis: attention speed, verbal memory), judge only after 12 weeks.
- **Interesting early signal (optional):** 5–15 ml MCT/virgin coconut oil in the morning — 2026 meta-analysis
  shows modest cognitive benefit from exogenous ketones.

- Tea/coffee ≥1 h away from meals (iron) · last caffeine 2 PM
- Water: ~3 L/day — 750 ml before the run, pale-straw urine by lunch
- 1 anthocyanin fruit daily (jamun / pomegranate / berries / amla)
