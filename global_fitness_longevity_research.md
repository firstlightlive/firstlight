## THE 5 BLUE ZONES — Where People Live to 100+

### 1. OKINAWA, JAPAN
- **Hara Hachi Bu**: Stop eating at 80% full (2500-year-old Confucian practice)
- **Diet**: Purple sweet potatoes (85% of calories), tofu, bitter melon, seaweed, turmeric
- **Moai**: Groups of 5 lifelong friends — meet daily, share everything
- **Ikigai**: Every elderly person has a clear purpose
- **Movement**: No gym — gardening, walking, light farm work daily
- **Warning**: Modern Okinawa adopted Western fast food — life expectancy DECLINING

### 2. SARDINIA, ITALY
- Only Blue Zone where men live as long as women
- **Shepherds walked 5+ miles daily** on steep rocky terrain
- **Cannonau wine**: 2-3x more flavonoids than other wines
- **Goat/sheep milk** (not cow — better fat profile, easier to digest)
- **Multigenerational households** — elderly never isolated

### 3. NICOYA, COSTA RICA
- Lowest income BUT highest lifespan (85 avg)
- **Hard water**: Highest calcium content in Costa Rica → stronger bones, less heart disease
- **Three Sisters diet**: Corn + beans + squash = complete protein
- **Plan de Vida**: Life plan — feeling needed by family

### 4. IKARIA, GREECE
- Live 10 years longer than Americans. Lowest dementia rates in the world
- **Herbal teas**: Wild rosemary, sage, oregano — natural diuretics + antioxidants
- **Daily napping**: 70% of 90+ year-olds nap daily → 35% lower heart disease
- **Late schedule**: Wake late, sleep late, nap afternoon — reduces cortisol

### 5. LOMA LINDA, CALIFORNIA
- Seventh-day Adventists live 10 years longer than average Americans
- **Vegetarian diet**: Nuts 5x/week = half heart disease risk
- **24-hour Sabbath**: No work, no screens, nature + family weekly
- **Church community** = built-in social network + accountability

## INDIA — What We Did Right & What We Lost

### Ancient Wisdom (What Worked)
- Dinacharya (daily routine), yoga, pranayama
- Spices: turmeric + black pepper (2000% better absorption)
- Fasting traditions (Ekadashi, Navratri)
- Ashwagandha, Triphala, Amla

### What Modern India Lost
| Problem | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| Sedentary shift | Agrarian → office in one generation |
| Processed food | Maida, sugar, packaged snacks explosion |
| Thrifty phenotype | Bodies store fat efficiently → diabetes at low BMI |
| Vitamin D deficiency | 8-10 hrs indoors despite sunny country |
| Air pollution | PM2.5 causes systemic inflammation |
| Sleep destruction | Screens, night shifts, no consistent schedule |
| Social isolation | Nuclear families replaced village support |

**CVD kills 26.6% of Indians. Indians die from heart disease a DECADE earlier than developed countries.**
