THE RULES
1 · THE SHUTDOWN — OFFLINE FIRST: normal close 7:00 PM, laptop in the bag, zipped. A war-day (planned, rare) may run to 9:00 PM — THE WALL. Nothing crosses it — the capital price stands behind it. THE LAPTOP'S TERRITORY: the office room, and nowhere else in the house — it enters no bedroom, no hall, no dining space; at 7:00 it goes into the bag, and the bag lives in the office room. The closing ritual every day: capture every open loop → plan tomorrow's blocks → say "Shutdown complete." A written plan releases the mind the way finishing does; the man who cannot stop is still clinging to the fruits. (Newport · Gita 2.47)
2 · THE SIX VAULTS — NOTHING PERSONAL, EVER: ① money (salary · trading P&L · purchases) ② family frictions ③ health ④ plans (job moves, ventures — announce AFTER, never before) ⑤ relationships ⑥ my verdict on any absent person. Warm but private: I am the best LISTENER in the room — I ask, remember, follow up; my openness is cricket, food, training, craft. Deflect-and-return: "All good — how did your migration land?" Never disclose self-doubt about my own competence at work — a senior man who does loses status and influence (proven); doubts go to the journal or a mentor outside the chain.
3 · ZERO GOSSIP ORIGINATED: I hear everything, I repeat nothing negative. About the absent I say one true good thing — "He's always been straight with me" — or the full stop: "I'd rather not talk about someone who isn't here." Feedback goes to the face, never around the back. The one who gossips TO me gossips ABOUT me — and everyone in the room knows it. Gossip originators are measurably rated less trustworthy, less likable, less expert.
4 · LEAD BEFORE THE TITLE (the 18-month campaign): ownership language — banned: "they didn't tell me," "not my area," "I was waiting on…"; replacement: "That's on me — here's the fix." · My boss hears bad news from ME first, always with a recommendation attached · Artifacts advertise: design docs, post-mortems, the Friday one-page note — the work is seen without my saying "I did this"; accomplishments stated plainly or not at all (humblebrag tests worse than silence) · In every meeting: speak EARLY, under 60 seconds, WITH a position · Credit given away by name — "Priya's analysis cracked it" · I am the thermostat, not the thermometer · ONE SPONSOR, two levels up: deliver on something they own, ask their advice on hard problems quarterly (advice-seeking RAISES perceived competence), make my name easy to say in rooms I am not in · Glue work refused politely; mission-central scope taken instead · I teach juniors on the clock — from this seat, that IS the audition.
5 · THE 50-HOUR CAP + INTEGRITY ABSOLUTE: output flatlines past ~50 sharp hours — the surplus goes to visibility, training people, and the missions; I never announce that I protect my time. Competence mistakes: confessed within 24 hours, fix attached — nearly free. Integrity is unrecoverable capital: one lie, one two-faced moment, defines me permanently. I say nothing behind a back I would not say to the face.

WHY — WHAT THE EVIDENCE ACTUALLY SAYS

One integrity failure is judged as a permanent trait; an owned mistake with a fix is nearly free. Gossip pays the originator in temporary belonging and charges him in permanent trust.
