MINIMUM VIABLE DAY × 3 · REBUILD ONE BLOCK A DAY · NO PUNISHMENTS IN COMEBACK MODE
I NEVER SAY "I FELL OFF." I SAY: "I AM RUNNING THE SMALL VERSION."
THE PROTOCOL
1 · DECLARE: two or more days of the system missed — for any reason: sickness, travel, work chaos, a life event, or plain collapse — and I write ONE line in the journal: "Comeback mode, Day 1." Declaring costs nothing. Hiding costs the system. The only true failure this book recognizes is staying collapsed in silence.
2 · THE MINIMUM VIABLE DAY — three things, nothing else required:
   ☐ WAKE by 6:00    ☐ WALK 20 minutes outside    ☐ LIGHTS OUT by 10:00
Everything else — study, swim, gym, market prep, rituals — is OPTIONAL and earns no guilt either way. The MVD is small on purpose: it must be doable on the worst day of my year, because that is exactly the day it exists for.
3 · THREE DAYS MINIMUM — no early upgrades: even if Day 2 feels magnificent, I stay small until Day 4. The eager Day-2 self who wants everything back at once is the same self who overbuilt the last fifty systems. Feeling strong on the small version is the fuel being stored, not the signal to spend it.
4 · REBUILD ONE BLOCK PER DAY, in this fixed order: ① sleep anchor (8:45 / 3:30) → ② the 6:00 training slot → ③ Study 1 → ④ everything else. Sleep first, always — it is the foundation the whole machine stands on. Full system restored in about a week, without a single heroic day.
5 · AMNESTY: no punishments accrue in comeback mode — no walks, no fasts, no penalty debt. The ledger marks these days "C", never zero. Years from now, the C-entries will be the proudest lines in the record: every one of them is a day the system refused to die.
6 · THE SICKNESS CLAUSE: fever, or any symptom below the neck → REST IS THE DISCIPLINE. Full stop, declared exemption, no miss, no penalty, no negotiation with the ego. Above-the-neck sniffles → easy movement only. Training through fever is not toughness; it is how athletes damage hearts.
THE PRICE OF A BREACH
This is the only rulebook with no penalties. Comeback mode is amnesty by design — a drowning man is not fined for swimming slowly.—
The single breach that exists: collapsing and NOT declaring. The fix for that breach is also free — declare now, late counts.
WHY — THE FIFTY
Fifty systems in fifteen years, and every single collapse was TOTAL — because nothing ever defined PARTIAL. July 2026: two bad nights became bedtimes at 2 AM, one run a month, a dead streak — not because the fall was big, but because there was nothing between "perfect" and "gone".

This page is the thing that was always missing: a floor. A written procedure that turns catastrophe into three checkboxes. The men who last are not the ones who never fall — they are the ones whose falls have a script.
