THE RULES
1 · THE DOORWAYS ARE ALREADY SEALED — I keep them sealed: porn requires three things at once — a screen, privacy, and a late hour. Rule 01 already outlawed all three: the phone lives in a drawer and never enters a bedroom or bathroom, the laptop is bagged at 7:00, the router dies at 8:15, no feeds exist on anything I own. This rule exists to finish the wall: family-filter DNS (NextDNS/CleanBrowsing) on the router and every device, its password written on paper and locked in the locked room. I never fight porn — I make it unreachable.
2 · THE BED IS FOR SLEEP. NOTHING ELSE HAPPENS THERE: never horizontal with any screen, anywhere on Earth · at 3:30 the alarm means FEET ON THE FLOOR — no lingering awake in bed, morning or night · the bathroom is for water, in and out. The relapse never begins at the act — it begins twenty minutes earlier, lying down, bored, alone. I do not enter the twenty minutes.
3 · THE 90-SECOND PROTOCOL — when the urge rises: the urge is a wave, not a command. It peaks and passes. I STAND UP → leave the room → cold water on the face → 10 slow breaths → 20 push-ups or straight out the door for a walk. I never negotiate with it sitting still, and NEVER lying down. Ride it standing and it dies in minutes — every single time it has ever been ridden.
4 · KNOW THE FOUR GATES — H.A.L.T.: Hungry · Angry · Lonely · Tired. Every past breach walked through one of these gates. When two are open at once, that is a RED ALERT evening: extra vigilance, earliest lights-out, the wind-down rituals done with full attention. Loneliness is the widest gate — the Sunday family call and one maintained friendship are Fortress defenses, not social niceties.
5 · TRANSMUTATION — the energy has a job: Ojas conserved is not energy suppressed, it is energy REDIRECTED — and my system is already the redirection: the 5 AM training, the cold water, Mula Bandha, the Brahmacharya mala at 4:02, the missions. A full day leaves nothing for the urge to feed on. The Fortress is not built at night; it is built at 3:30 AM.
6 · WITNESSED, AS ALWAYS: the FORTRESS line in the daily check-in (CLEAN / BREACH), urge intensity logged 1–10 — a logged urge is a defused urge. Streak counted. Sunday review, in pen.
