A CONSTITUTION'S POWER IS ITS AGE
THE LAW
1 · SUNDAY ONLY: a rule may be changed only at the Sunday weekly review, in writing, on the review sheet. Never verbally, never "from tomorrow", never at night. The version of me that writes laws works on Sunday mornings; every other version of me only OBEYS them.
2 · ONE AMENDMENT PER WEEK, MAXIMUM. If three things feel wrong, I fix the biggest one and live with the rest for seven more days. Most complaints about a rule dissolve before their Sunday arrives — that is the filter working.
3 · THE 48-HOUR COOLING LAW: no rule may be touched within 48 hours of my breaking it. The man who just broke a rule and wants it "adjusted" is not the lawmaker — he is the addict negotiating. He gets heard on Sunday, sober, in writing, never at the scene of the breach.
4 · THE FREEZE: Rules 01 (Phone) and 02 (Office) are FROZEN until 22 NOVEMBER 2026 — 90 days untouched. Every week a rule survives unedited it gains the authority that willpower never had. New rules enter as DRAFTS and are signed only after surviving one full week unmodified.
5 · THE CAP — TEN RULES, NO MORE: the constitution is complete at Rules 01–10 plus this page. A man with forty rules has none. If a new rule truly must exist, an old one must retire the same Sunday.
6 · THE PENALTY CEILING: maximum 50 penalty-km outstanding in any week, across all rulebooks. Debt beyond that is not paid by more walking — it triggers a Sunday SYSTEM REVIEW, because heavy penalty-debt means a rule is broken, not a man. Penalties never compound. Ever.
THE PRICE OF A BREACH
A rule edited off-Sunday, or inside the 48-h cooldownTHE CHANGE IS VOID + 30 KM.
A new rule adopted without its one-week draftVOID. REDRAFT.
Paid within 24 hours, daylight only, logged in pen on Sunday — same terms as every book.
WHY — MY OWN NUMBERS
Rule 01 went through 27 versions in two days. The training plan, five rewrites in three. And the fifteen-year record reads: fifty systems, fifty collapses. The pattern is not weak discipline — my systems have never been broken by failing to follow them; they have been broken by redesigning them. Rebuilding gives the same dopamine the scroll gave, dressed in virtue.

So this page exists to protect the constitution from its author. I do not need more rules. I need my rules to grow old.
