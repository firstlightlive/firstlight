## PART 1 — LEARNING SCIENCE (the two deep-work missions)

1. **Blank-editor re-solve queue (STRONG — the biggest effect in all of learning science).** Retrieval beats re-reading at g≈0.5–0.6 (Adesope 2017, 217 effects). Every coding problem re-solved COLD at 1–2 d → 1 wk → 3 wk; retired only after two clean cold solves ≥1 wk apart ("successive relearning": count criterion hits, not reviews → ~83% retention at 30 days). Anki only for the declarative layer (complexity facts, system-design numbers, pattern cues).
2. **The 90-min block structure (STRONG):** 4:15–4:25 recall-first brain dump of yesterday (rides out sleep inertia) → 4:25–4:30 pretest today's topic (prequestion g=0.66) → 4:30–5:20 main work (new pattern via worked examples OR timed mixed set, alternating days) → 5:20–5:35 spaced re-solve queue → 5:35–5:45 error log + 2-min reflection.
3. **Interleaving with hidden tags (MODERATE, g=0.34–0.42):** mixed problem sets force "WHICH technique is this?" — the actual interview skill. New patterns get a brief blocked worked-example phase first.
4. **Think-aloud + observed mocks (the biggest untrained gap):** candidates watched by an interviewer score ~HALF their private performance (Behroozi/NC State). Verbalize EVERY practice problem; weekly mock with an observer — an AI interviewer at 4:15 AM works (AI-tutor RCT d=0.73–1.3; rule: AI as interviewer/reviewer, never as solver first). 10 min expressive writing before real interviews kills choking (Ramirez & Beilock, Science).
5. **Error log with error-management framing (d=0.80 on adaptive transfer):** classify every miss — comprehension / pattern-ID / implementation / communication.
6. **Hand-draw system-design diagrams from memory** (generation + dual coding + retrieval; typed notes for code — task-authentic).
7. **Trading psychology (MODERATE, honest):** the only causal-evidence items — (a) pre-market checklist ≤7 binary items; (b) **loss-day auto-rule: morning drawdown past threshold → HALF position size all afternoon, pre-committed** (Coval-Shumway: morning losses cause afternoon over-risking); (c) 3:05 PM = 15 min WRITTEN process reflection first (Di Stefano field RCT: +23%); (d) label the emotion at entry (1 word + arousal 1–10). Nothing repeals the Barber-Odean base rate — these cut unforced errors, they don't create edge.
8. **Sleep is the binding constraint (STRONG):** 14 days at 6h ≈ 1–2 nights total deprivation, cumulative, and *subjectively invisible* (Van Dongen 2003). Regularity is protective. Spaced retrieval is the technique most robust to weak single-night consolidation. Before real interviews: +45–60 min sleep for 2–3 nights measurably restores encoding.

## PART 2 — RECOVERY & DURABILITY

1. **RUN-SPIKE CAP (supersedes the 10% rule).** Garmin-RUNSAFE (5,205 runners): exceeding your longest run of the past 30 days by 10–30% = +64% injury risk; >100% = +128%. **Rule: no run >110% of the longest run in the past 30 days — race days included.** Consequence: HM Sep 27 off a 16k longest = +32% spike → either build long run toward ~19k or race it as a training run (walk breaks, finish mode). Injury onset is sudden, not gradual.
2. **Heat block Jan 18 → Feb 14 (STRONG — the standout new finding):** heat training raises hemoglobin mass +3–4% and VO2max — "free altitude" in India. 10–14 controlled-easy heat sessions (no fan, layers, or midday), then 2×/wk maintenance; last one 2–3 days pre-70.3. Suspend cold-water immersion during the block. Decay ~2.5%/day.
3. **The 90-second morning panel (STRONG — wearable "readiness scores" failed validation, 2025 review of 10 brands):** RHR 7-day trend (flag +5 bpm sustained) · HR *and* RPE on one fixed-effort segment (killer signal: HR flat, RPE up, pace down) · 1-min HRR · 4-item wellness (sleep/soreness/mood/stress) · weekly weight (>2% drop = under-fuelling). Optional: morning lnRMSSD 7-day rolling with SWC gate — modifies intensity days only, never the long run.
4. **Sleep-efficiency stack (duration is fixed → optimize quality):** hot shower/bath 40–42°C ≥10 min at ~7:00 PM (−8.6 min onset, more deep sleep) · bedroom 20–22°C with airflow, CO2 <1000 ppm · dim warm light from ~6 PM, <10 lux final hour · **caffeine cutoff 12:00 NOON** (tightened from 2 PM — Gardiner 8.8h rule vs 8:45 bed) · zero alcohol in build blocks · Fri/Sat drift ≤30 min.
5. **Durability additions:** rotate 2–3 shoe models (39% lower injury, Malisoux) · plyometrics 2×/wk on gym days, 60–100 contacts, pogo/ankle-stiffness first (plyo+resistance = large running-economy effect) · weekly 6–10 × 20–30s hill sprints · compression socks 4–8 h after every long run · CWI never on gym days (blunts strength adaptation), fine after hot long rides and races.
6. **Masters asymmetry:** swims/rides = cheap volume; running/eccentric = the expensive sessions — space hard running ≥72 h, heavy legs ≥24–48 h from the long run. Trust the wellness score over the watch (perceived recovery lags at 40+). Baseline cardiac screen (ECG/echo) before the full-IM build.
7. Modality grades: compression garments MODERATE (best of the gadgets) · foam roll pre-run 5 min · massage gun = perceptual only · sauna 3–4×/wk post-easy sessions (+32% TTE via plasma volume; 40°C bath 30–40 min = equivalent).

## PART 3 — ENVIRONMENT

1. **CO2 + the 4:00 AM flush (STRONG):** complex cognition degrades from ~1,000–1,500 ppm; a closed bedroom/study hits 1,500–2,500 ppm by 3:30 AM — the hardest block runs in the day's worst air. 5-min cross-ventilation flush at 4:00, door ajar, re-flush at sunlight breaks. NDIR CO2 monitor (Aranet4 / Qingping / Prana Air), alarm 1,000–1,200. Plants do NOT clean air (need 10–1,000/m²). HEPA purifier for Bangalore PM2.5; run it in the study during cook hour.
2. **Light protocol (STRONG):** 4:15 AM = bright cool 4000–6500 K desk lamp, ~250–500 lux at the eye (phase-ADVANCES — anchors the early wake, can't hurt it). Evening: warm 2700 K <50 lux from ~6 PM, <10 lux final hour (melatonin suppression starts under 30 lux). Skip the 10,000-lux SAD lamp.
3. **Movement snacks 9:00–3:00 (STRONG — best-evidenced desk intervention):** every 30–45 min, 60–120 s (squats/calf raises/corridor walk) → postprandial glucose −30–40%; the non-negotiable one: 3–5 min walk right after lunch. Seated calf raises during live positions. Use the 11:30–1:30 market lull for the longest break.
4. **Cook-hour containment (7:50–8:50):** door closed + kitchen exhaust + purifier; ANC headphones only if speech stays intelligible (ANC has no cognitive-performance effect — it's a speech-masking tool).
5. **Temperature:** 26–28°C with a good fan (BLDC) is evidence-clean for Bangalore; AC only above ~28. The 4:15 block at 19–22°C ambient is already optimal.
6. **Verdicts to skip:** blue-light glasses (Cochrane: no difference) · plants as purifiers · binaural beats/Mozart · lyrics during study (g≈−0.2; instrumental neutral) · brown-noise apps (helps ADHD profiles only, impairs others).
