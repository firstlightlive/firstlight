Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

FirstLight ("we," "us," or "our") operates the firstlight.live website and app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information.

2. Data Collection

FirstLight collects:

  • Instagram activity data (with your authorization)
  • Strava running/fitness data (with your authorization)
  • Personal fitness metrics you log
  • Account authentication data

3. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to:

  • Display your fitness activities and progress
  • Calculate streaks and accountability metrics
  • Generate reports and analytics
  • Maintain account security

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on Supabase (https://supabase.com). We do not sell or share your data with third parties.

5. User Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your data at any time. We will delete all stored data within 30 days of your request. To request deletion, email: firstlightlive@gmail.com

6. Third-Party Services

FirstLight integrates with:

  • Instagram (Meta) — for activity publishing

  • Cloudflare — for hosting
  • Supabase — for data storage
